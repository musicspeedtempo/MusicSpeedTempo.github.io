01. Publisher and purpose

These terms concern the Android app Speedio - Music Speed Tempo (package: pl.musictempo.app), published by Robert Kruk. Contact: MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com.

Speedio is a music practice tool for people aged 13 and older. It lets you open supported music files and links, adjust playback speed, repeat an A–B fragment and save playlists and playback history on your device. Available functions depend on the selected source.

02. Your music and chosen sources

Use files and services that you are entitled to access, respecting copyright and the source provider's terms. Access to a link or a file does not grant additional rights to copy, distribute or publish the music. You choose which files, links and backups to open or share.

Keep original music and any exported library backup in a suitable location. The backup contains library information, not the music itself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes its private library and preferences; separate music files and exported backups are not removed.

03. YouTube Terms of Service

Speedio uses YouTube API Services through the official embedded player. By using Speedio's YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's age and eligibility requirements also apply, including permission from a parent or guardian where required.

The player remains visible. Speedio does not download YouTube videos, extract separate audio files or provide access around YouTube's restrictions. YouTube controls the availability of videos, available playback speeds, regional and age restrictions, and its own advertising. A video may be unavailable or stop working in the embedded player.

Google's processing is explained in the Google Privacy Policy and in Speedio's privacy policy. Speedio does not offer YouTube sign-in or request OAuth access to your account.

04. Acceptance and privacy choices

Before loading YouTube, the app asks you to accept these terms and the privacy policy. You can read both documents offline. You can decline and continue using local music files.

The accepted versions of both documents and the acceptance timestamp are saved only on your device, not on a publisher server. You can withdraw acceptance from the app menu. This stops and closes the YouTube player without deleting your library or playlists; using YouTube again requires acceptance. Withdrawal does not remove data already received by Google or other providers.

Acceptance of these documents does not change Google's separate privacy settings and is not consent to personalized advertising. See the privacy policy for processing details, advertising choices where available, retention and deletion requests.

05. Playback and external service availability

Supported file formats depend on the device's decoders. Online playback depends on your connection and the source provider. Speed changes and A–B loops may be limited by the source; buffering or seeking can interrupt a loop. External providers may change or remove content or restrict embedding.

If you experience a problem, contact MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com and describe what happened. Please do not send music files or unnecessary personal information. These terms do not exclude rights you have under applicable law.

06. Changes and access to the documents

The terms and privacy policy remain available from the app menu. Their versions and revision dates appear at the start. If an app update requires acceptance of revised documents, the app asks before loading YouTube again. You may decline and continue using local music files.
