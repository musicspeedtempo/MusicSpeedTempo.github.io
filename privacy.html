01. Controller and contact

This policy covers the Android app Speedio - Music Speed Tempo (package: pl.musictempo.app) and its information website. The app is published by Robert Kruk, who is also the controller for personal data processed by the publisher. Privacy contact: MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com.

Google, YouTube, GitHub and the providers of sources you choose may process information through their own services as described below.

02. Music files, playlists and history

Speedio reads files you select and their metadata to play music, adjust speed and display the waveform. You grant access through the Android system document picker. If you select a cloud document, its provider may retrieve it over the network.

On your device, we store titles, file addresses and links, YouTube video identifiers, playlists, recent playback history, the last opened track and playback settings: position, speed, A–B points and loop locking. These let you return to your practice.

The app does not require an account and does not upload music files or your entire library to a publisher server. It has no publisher-operated cloud sync or separate publisher analytics or crash-reporting module. Google services, including YouTube and Android WebView, may perform the processing described below.

We also store, only on your device, the versions of the privacy policy and terms you accepted and the acceptance timestamp. This records whether the current YouTube requirements have been accepted; it is not sent to a publisher server.

03. Online sources, YouTube and WebView

Opening an online source connects you to its provider. The provider receives information such as your IP address, the requested resource address and technical connection details. A complete link may contain an identifier or access token. Data handling also depends on the service you choose.

Your choice before using YouTube

Before loading YouTube, Speedio asks you to accept this privacy policy and the terms of use, which include the YouTube Terms of Service. You can decline and continue using local music files. You can withdraw acceptance from the app menu; this stops and closes the YouTube player without deleting your library or playlists. YouTube remains unavailable until you accept again. Withdrawal does not erase information already received by external providers.

This acceptance does not change Google's separate privacy settings and is not consent to personalized advertising.

YouTube's embedded player

The YouTube feature uses YouTube API Services and an embedded player. Google receives information needed to operate it, such as the video identifier, IP address and playback information. Basic information is sent when the player loads, even before playback; further information may be sent during playback. YouTube may serve its own content and ads and store or read cookies and similar information on your device, including browser or device identifiers. Google may use your IP address to estimate your general location. Its processing can support player operation, analytics, advertising, personalization, security and fraud prevention, as described in Google's information about partner sites and apps.

Speedio ad settings do not control YouTube ads. The YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply. Speedio does not offer YouTube account sign-in or OAuth access to your account.

WebView diagnostics and crash reports

The player uses Android WebView. If you enable sharing usage and diagnostics with Google, WebView may send Google statistics and crash reports. These can include system, feature and performance information, and app stack memory needed to diagnose a WebView crash. Statistics and crash reports use separate random, per-app 128-bit pseudonymous tokens. Google uses reporting to improve WebView and diagnose problems. You can change Google's Android “Usage & diagnostics” setting; this does not control YouTube's separate processing. See WebView reporting and Google's setting help.

04. Advertising and privacy choices

Speedio banner ads are disabled in the current production release. Test builds may enable test banners. YouTube can still display its own ads independently.

This section applies when advertising is enabled in your version of the app. We use Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle privacy messages and choices.

The Google advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (including to estimate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers where available. These support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See How Google uses information from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

UMP communicates with Google to determine applicable messages and manage saved choices. Consent information may be refreshed when the app starts. The absence of a visible banner does not mean that these connections are absent.

Where required by applicable rules and service configuration, a form lets you manage consent. You can return to available controls through “Ad and consent settings” in the menu. Declining personalization does not mean that advertising or security measurement processes no information. We do not send library contents or music files to AdMob to target advertising.

05. Age and younger users

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. In versions with advertising, you can choose 13–17, 18 or older, or “Skip”. We do not ask for a date of birth. Your choice is stored on the device; the resulting age-restriction setting is passed to Google advertising services.

For ages 13–17, we enable treatment as a user under the age of consent for advertising and disable personalization. Google may consequently not display a consent form. Choosing “Skip” lets you use the app's features without Speedio banner ads. You can change this in the age settings menu. This does not erase information previously received by providers or change the rules of services such as YouTube.

06. Manual library backups

Export creates a JSON file with library metadata, playlists, history and playback settings. It includes complete source addresses, but no music files or file-access permissions. Speedio does not encrypt this backup.

You choose where to save a backup and which file to import. If you choose cloud storage or share the backup, its data goes to that provider or recipient. Automatic app-data backup through Android Backup is disabled.

07. Messages to the publisher

If you email MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, attachments and other information you choose to provide. We use these to handle the request, communicate and resolve problems. Email is handled through Google Gmail. Please do not send music files or personal information that is unnecessary to resolve the request.

08. Purposes and legal bases

Where we process personal data and the GDPR applies:

  • providing requested features and related support relies on performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b));
  • other correspondence, security and establishing or defending claims rely on the controller's legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f));
  • personalized advertising and access to device information that requires consent rely on consent (Article 6(1)(a) and applicable device-storage rules);
  • processing required by law relies on a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)), where applicable.

Providing contact information is voluntary, but we may be unable to reply without it. Consent to personalized advertising is not required to use music features. The controller does not make solely automated decisions producing legal effects; Google may personalize ads according to your settings and its own policy.

09. Retention and deletion

  • Acceptance record: document versions and the acceptance timestamp are stored locally. You can withdraw acceptance from the app menu. Clearing app data or uninstalling also removes this local record.
  • Library and preferences: remain on the device until removed. Removing a track from the library does not delete its music file. Deleting a playlist does not delete the tracks.
  • All local app data: use Android Settings → Apps → Speedio → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Labels vary by device. This removes private app data, including its local WebView data and preferences.
  • Exported backups and original music: remain in their chosen locations and must be deleted separately. Uninstalling Speedio does not remove them.
  • Correspondence: we retain it for as long as needed to resolve the matter, and longer only as necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend claims.
  • External services: their providers' retention rules apply. Uninstalling the app does not remove information from Google, YouTube, GitHub or source-provider servers.

10. Recipients, security and transfers

Recipients may include the playback, WebView diagnostics, advertising, consent, email and hosting providers described above, and file services you select. Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities when required by law. We do not provide your music files or playlists to advertisers.

App data is kept in Android's private app storage. Google advertising services encrypt data in transit. However, a music link you provide may use unencrypted HTTP; prefer HTTPS sources. Choosing appropriate recipients and storage locations also protects your manual backups and messages.

International providers may process information outside the EEA. Depending on the service, safeguards may include adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Details and information about safeguards are available in Google’s data-transfer information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Data deletion and your rights

Request data deletion — Speedio - Music Speed Tempo

Publisher: Robert Kruk. You can send a request without creating an account or installing the app.

  • Email MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com. You can use the subject “Speedio — data deletion”.
  • Describe which data you want deleted, such as earlier support messages and attachments. If possible, write from the address used in that correspondence and give its approximate date. Do not send your entire library or music files.

We will reply to your request. If we need additional information to locate the data or confirm that it concerns you, we will explain what is needed. We will also let you know if we do not hold the specified data.

  • Data deleted by the publisher: to the extent your request is granted, we will delete correspondence we hold about you, including your email address, message content, attachments and other information supplied in it, subject to the exceptions below.
  • Data retained and retention periods: we may retain only information necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend claims, such as the relevant part of a message. The period depends on the applicable legal obligation or the time needed to handle a claim, taking applicable limitation periods into account. Our reply will explain the scope, reason and duration of any such retention, or the criteria used to determine it.
  • Data on your phone: the publisher has no remote access to playlists, history, preferences or local WebView data. You can remove these through Android Settings → Apps → Speedio → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Original music files and exported backups remain in their chosen locations; delete them separately if you wish.
  • Data held by external providers: emailing the publisher does not automatically delete information independently held by Google, YouTube, GitHub or source providers. Use the tools and contact channels described in their policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. Those providers determine their data retention periods.

General retention practices are also described under “Retention and deletion”.

Other rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Send requests concerning the publisher to MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com; advertising consent controls are also available in the app where provided by the service.

You may complain to a data protection authority, including Poland's Personal Data Protection Office. We do not have remote access to your library; you remove its data locally. For information independently held by an external provider, you can also use that provider's tools and contact channels.

12. Offline documents and information website

The privacy policy and terms included in the app are displayed locally without downloading web content. Opening an external link or choosing an email link uses the selected browser or email service.

The Speedio information website adds no cookies, advertising or analytics tools of its own and does not embed YouTube. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Policy updates

We update this policy when app features, services or applicable requirements change. The document version and revision date appear at the start. If an app update requires acceptance of a new document version, Speedio asks before loading YouTube again. Publication alone does not replace additional notice or consent where required.

The terms of use are available alongside this policy in the app. Questions: MusicSpeedTempo@gmail.com.
