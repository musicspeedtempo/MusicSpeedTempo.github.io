Speedio – Music Speed Tempo

Practice at your own pace.

Slow down music without changing pitch. Speedio is a music practice player for Android, built for guitarists and other musicians learning by ear.

Get it on Google Play

From a riff to a full solo

Set points A and B, name your loop and return to it next time you practice. Speedio estimates tempo (BPM) and key for local audio files. Open supported links, including YouTube, and organize songs into playlists.

Learn a fast solo one phrase at a time

Open a recording and slow down a tricky bar until you can hear the order of the notes. The pitch stays the same, so you can play along in the original tuning. Once a few repetitions feel even, raise the tempo and listen for where the last note lands against the beat.

Return to the same bar next time

Spread two fingers on the timeline to place the start and end of your A–B loop precisely. Lock the points while you seek through the track. Save the phrase as “Solo intro”. Next time, open the song from Recently Played or a playlist and select the saved loop.
